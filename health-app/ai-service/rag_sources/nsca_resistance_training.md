# Resistance training principles

Resistance training should target major movement patterns and progress by volume, load, or difficulty over time. Beginners can use bodyweight, bands, or dumbbells with controlled tempo. Leaving 2-3 reps in reserve is a practical way to avoid excessive fatigue while still training effectively.
