# Recovery after exercise

Recovery helps adaptation and reduces overuse risk. Sleep, hydration, lighter days, mobility work, and alternating training stress can help users respond to exercise. If recent sleep is low or today already has high training volume, recovery or mobility can be a better recommendation than another hard workout.
