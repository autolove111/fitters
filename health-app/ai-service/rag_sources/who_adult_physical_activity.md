# Adult physical activity

Adults benefit from 150-300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week, or a smaller amount of vigorous activity. Muscle-strengthening work should be included on 2+ days each week. People who are currently inactive should start with manageable sessions and increase duration before increasing intensity.
