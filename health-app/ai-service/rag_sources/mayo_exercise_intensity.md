# Exercise intensity

Intensity can be estimated with breathing, heart rate, perceived exertion, and the talk test. Beginners should be able to speak during moderate exercise and should avoid pushing through warning symptoms. A plan can use low-to-moderate intensity when sleep is poor, soreness is high, or injury risk is present.
