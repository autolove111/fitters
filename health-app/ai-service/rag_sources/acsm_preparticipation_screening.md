# Exercise safety screening

Exercise planning should account for known disease, symptoms, current activity level, and pain. Users reporting knee discomfort, joint pain, chest symptoms, dizziness, or unusual shortness of breath should keep intensity conservative and stop training if symptoms increase. Professional evaluation is appropriate when warning signs appear.
