# Exercise selection

Exercise selection should match available equipment and the user's current ability. Bodyweight, resistance bands, and dumbbells can cover squat, hinge, push, pull, carry, and core patterns. If a movement causes pain, reduce range of motion or switch to a lower-impact alternative.
